# Category Policy Addendum: Beauty

# KARTWAY OPERATIONAL POLICY HANDBOOK
## ADDENDUM 4.2: BEAUTY CATEGORY PROTOCOLS
**Effective Date:** 1 January 2026  
**Document Owner:** Priya Nair, Head of Customer Operations  
**Applicability:** All Tier-1 and Tier-2 Support Agents handling Beauty category orders.

---

### 4.2.1 Return Window
1. **Standard Window:** The return window for all items listed in the Beauty category is exactly **10** days from the date of confirmed delivery.
2. **Late Submissions:** Return requests initiated on day **11** or later from delivery shall be automatically blocked by the system and must be rejected by agents without exception.

### 4.2.2 Condition Requirements & Packaging
1. **Original Packaging:** Original packaging is mandatory for all returns in the Beauty category. The return must include all original manufacturer boxes, outer wrappers, and tamper-evident seals.
2. **Seal Integrity:** The seal must be intact for any return to be accepted. If the seal is broken, torn, or missing, the item is classified as opened.
3. **Change of Mind Restriction:** An opened item cannot be returned for a change of mind. 

### 4.2.3 Restocking Fees and Return Pickup Fees
1. **Restocking Fee:** Because opened items are ineligible for change-of-mind returns, no restocking fee exists for this category.
2. **Change of Mind Logistics Fee:** For authorized change-of-mind returns of unopened items, the return pickup fee is exactly **99** rupees. This fee shall be directly deducted from the total refund amount processed to the customer.

### 4.2.4 Damaged, Defective, or Incorrect Items
1. **Reporting Deadline:** A damaged, wrong, or defective item must be reported within exactly **10** days of delivery.
2. **Refund Terms:** Valid claims under this section shall be refunded in full with no fee deducted. The **99** rupees pickup fee is waived for damaged, wrong, or defective items.

### 4.2.5 Hazardous and Perishable Handling Rules
1. **Flammable Classification:** Aerosols, perfume, and nail polish are classified as flammable goods.
2. **Transport Method:** Flammable beauty items are collected by surface transport only. Air freight return pickups must not be scheduled for these sub-categories under any circumstances.

### 4.2.6 Warranty Position
1. **Category Warranty:** No warranty applies to this category. Kartway and its third-party sellers offer zero repair, replacement, or service cover for Beauty items post-delivery.
2. **Listing Errors:** A warranty period shown on a listing in this category is a listing error and gives no cover. Agents must cite this policy clause and refuse any post-delivery warranty claims regardless of what text appeared on the product display page.
