# Category Policy Addendum: Apparel

# KARTWAY OPERATIONAL POLICY MANUAL
## ADDENDUM AP-04: APPAREL CATEGORY RULES
**Effective Date:** 14 January 2025  
**Applies To:** All Tier-1 and Tier-2 Support Agents handling Marketplace Orders  
**Supersedes:** General Returns Policy (Section 3) for SKU Class 400–499  

---

### 1.0 PURPOSE AND SCOPE
**1.1** This document establishes the category-specific operational rules for Apparel items sold on the Kartway marketplace. Where rules in this addendum conflict with the General Operations Manual, the clauses herein take precedence for all items classified under Apparel.

---

### 2.0 RETURN WINDOWS AND PICKUP FEES
**2.1** The standard return window for any Apparel item is exactly **45** calendar days from the recorded delivery date.  
**2.2** For change-of-mind returns, the return pickup service is completely free of charge to the customer, incurring a fee of **$0.00**.  
**2.3** Return requests initiated after the expiration of the **45**-day window must be automatically rejected by support agents without exception.

---

### 3.0 CONDITION REQUIREMENTS AND CATEGORY EXCLUSIONS
**3.1** To be eligible for a standard return, Apparel items must satisfy the following strict condition requirements:
*   Items must be completely unworn.
*   Items must be completely unwashed.
*   Original manufacturer tags must remain attached to the item.

**3.2** Innerwear and swimwear items are strictly non-returnable for change-of-mind reasons and are returnable only if proven to be defective upon arrival.  
**3.3** If an innerwear or swimwear item is returned outside of a defect claim, the agent must reject the return and apply a processing denial code **AP-RET-09**.

---

### 4.0 PACKAGING AND RESTOCKING FEES
**4.1** The inclusion of original packaging (such as brand boxes, tissue paper, or dust bags) is **not** mandatory for an Apparel return to be accepted.  
**4.2** Kartway applies a restocking fee of **0%** (zero percent) for all processed Apparel returns. No monetary deductions shall be made from customer refunds for restocking.

---

### 5.0 DEFECTIVE, DAMAGED, OR INCORRECT ITEMS
**5.1** Any Apparel item received by a customer that is damaged, incorrect, or defective must be formally reported to Kartway support within **45** days of the recorded delivery date.  
**5.2** Validated claims for damaged, wrong, or defective items shall be refunded in full, consisting of **100%** of the purchase price and initial shipping fees, with **$0.00** in administrative or return fees deducted.

---

### 6.0 HAZARDOUS AND PERISHABLE HANDLING RULES
**6.1** Standard Apparel items contain no hazardous chemicals, biological hazards, or perishable components and require no special handling quarantine.  
**6.2** Any Apparel item returned bearing biological contaminants (such as bodily fluids or severe odors) violates the unwashed/unworn clause (Section 3.1) and must be categorized as biohazardous, resulting in immediate disposal, zero refund, and customer account flagging under code **SEC-BIO-02**.

---

### 7.0 WARRANTY POSITION
**7.1** Exactly **0** (zero) months or days of warranty apply to the Apparel category. No manufacturer or marketplace warranty coverage exists for normal wear and tear, manufacturing flaws discovered after use, or durability issues.  
**7.2** If a warranty period or guarantee is displayed on a marketplace listing within the Apparel category, support agents must treat this strictly as a listing error. Such text confers **0** days of coverage and provides the customer with no entitlement to repair, replacement, or warranty-based refund.
