# Kartway Returns and Refunds Policy

**KARTWAY CUSTOMER OPERATIONS POLICY HANDBOOK**  
**SECTION 2: RETURNS, REFUNDS, AND CANCELLATIONS**

**2.1 Return Windows by Category**  
2.1.1 The standard return window is 30 days, counted strictly from the delivery date recorded by the carrier.  
2.1.2 Category-specific return windows apply as follows:  
(a) Electronics: 15 days from the delivery date.  
(b) Home & Kitchen: 30 days from the delivery date.  
(c) Apparel: 45 days from the delivery date.  
(d) Beauty: 10 days from the delivery date.  
(e) Sports & Outdoors: 30 days from the delivery date.  
(f) Toys: 20 days from the delivery date.  
(g) Groceries: Non-returnable under all circumstances.  
(h) Furniture: 7 days from the delivery date.  

**2.2 General Condition Requirements**  
2.2.1 To be eligible for a return, items must be in their original packaging with all tags, manuals, and accessories intact.  
2.2.2 Items showing signs of misuse, unauthorized repair, or missing components outside of permitted inspection shall be rejected by the Kartway returns processing center.  

**2.3 Refund Calculation Basis**  
2.3.1 Every refund is calculated exclusively on the actual amount the customer paid for the order.  
2.3.2 Refunds shall never be calculated on the current list price or future promotional pricing of the item.  

**2.4 Refund Reasons, Payouts, and Return Shipping Allocations**  
2.4.1 **damaged:** The item arrived damaged and was reported, accompanied by a photographic proof, within the category's defect claim period. For Groceries, the refund is processed on the photo without requiring a physical return. Refund: 100% of the amount paid. Return shipping: Paid by Kartway.  
2.4.2 **wrong_item:** A different item from the one ordered was delivered. Refund: 100% of the amount paid. Return shipping: Paid by Kartway.  
2.4.3 **quality:** The item is defective or not as described. Refund: 100% of the amount paid. Return shipping: Paid by Kartway.  
2.4.4 **not_delivered:** The parcel was declared lost in transit, or a delivery dispute was upheld by the logistics team. There is nothing to return. Refund: 100% of the amount paid.  
2.4.5 **late_delivery:** The order arrived after the promised delivery date and the customer refuses delivery or returns the item upon receipt. A customer who keeps the order receives the delay credit instead of a return. Refund: 100% of the amount paid. Return shipping: Paid by Kartway.  
2.4.6 **changed_mind:** The customer no longer wants the item. Returns under this reason are accepted only inside the category's specified return window. Refund: 100% of the amount paid, less the category restocking fee (if the item was opened) and less the return pickup fee. Return shipping: Paid for by the customer through the deduction of the pickup fee.  
2.4.7 **cancelled_before_dispatch:** The order was successfully cancelled before it shipped from the fulfillment center. There is nothing to return. Refund: 100% of the amount paid.  

**2.5 Restocking Fees for Opened Items (Change of Mind)**  
2.5.1 If an item is returned due to a change of mind and has been opened, a restocking fee is charged as a percentage of the amount paid, rounded to the nearest rupee:  
(a) Electronics: 15%  
(b) Home & Kitchen: 10%  
(c) Apparel: No restocking fee (0%)  
(d) Beauty: Opened items are strictly not returnable for a change of mind.  
(e) Sports & Outdoors: 12%  
(f) Toys: 5%  
(g) Furniture: 20%  

**2.6 Return Pickup Fees (Change of Mind)**  
2.6.1 A standard return pickup fee of 99 rupees applies to change-of-mind returns and is deducted directly from the refund amount.  
2.6.2 Exceptions to the pickup fee are:  
(a) Apparel: Pickup fee is nil (free return pickup).  
(b) Furniture: Pickup fee is 499 rupees.  

**2.7 Refund Processing Times by Payment Method**  
2.7.1 Once a return or cancellation is formally approved by Kartway operations, funds are disbursed within the following timelines:  
(a) card: 5 to 7 business days, credited to the original payment card.  
(b) upi: 3 business days, credited to the source UPI account.  
(c) wallet: 24 hours, credited to the Kartway customer wallet.  
(d) cod: 10 business days, transferred to the bank account provided by the customer via the secure payout portal.  
(e) netbanking: 5 to 7 business days, credited to the source bank account.  

**2.8 Worked Example: Opened Home & Kitchen Item (Change of Mind)**  
2.8.1 Support agents shall calculate deductions using the following mandatory formula for opened change-of-mind returns.  
2.8.2 Example calculation for a Home & Kitchen item purchased for 8,000 rupees, returned opened due to a change of mind:  
(a) Amount paid: 8,000 rupees.  
(b) Category restocking fee (10% of 8,000 rupees): 800 rupees.  
(c) Return pickup fee: 99 rupees.  
(d) Total deductions (800 + 99): 899 rupees.  
(e) Final refund issued to customer (8,000 - 899): 7,101 rupees.
