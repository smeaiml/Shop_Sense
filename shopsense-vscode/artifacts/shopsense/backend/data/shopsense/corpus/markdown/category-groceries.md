# Category Policy Addendum: Groceries

# KARTWAY OPERATIONAL POLICY MANUAL
## ADDENDUM 4.2: GROCERIES CATEGORY RULES
**Effective Date:** 14 January 2025  
**Applies To:** All Tier-1 and Tier-2 Support Agents, Dispute Resolution Specialists, and Automated Refund Engines  
**Document Classification:** Internal Operational Standard  

---

### 1.0 PURPOSE AND SCOPE
This document establishes the binding standard operating procedures for the Groceries category on the Kartway marketplace. In the event of a conflict between this addendum and the general Kartway Customer Operations Policy, the specific rules contained within this document shall supersede all general guidelines.

### 2.0 RETURN WINDOW AND CHANGE-OF-MIND RESTRICTIONS
* **2.1 Non-Returnable Classification:** The Groceries category is classified as strictly non-returnable. No change-of-mind returns are accepted under any circumstances. Agents must reject any return request where the customer states they no longer want, require, or have ordered the item in error.
* **2.2 Exception Window for Discrepancies:** Notwithstanding Section 2.1, claims relating to items that arrive damaged, wrong, or defective must be reported to Kartway Customer Operations strictly within **2** days of the recorded delivery date. Claims submitted after **2** days from delivery shall be automatically rejected by the system.

### 3.0 CONDITION REQUIREMENTS AND PACKAGING
* **3.1 Item Condition:** Items reported under Section 2.2 must be verified via customer-submitted evidence in accordance with Section 5.1.
* **3.2 Packaging Independence:** Original packaging is **not** mandatory for the processing of refunds under this category addendum. Agents must not require customers to retain or return outer boxes, wrappers, or factory seals in order to qualify for a refund on damaged, wrong, or defective grocery items.

### 4.0 RESTOCKING FEES
* **4.1 Fee Structure:** Because no change-of-mind returns are accepted, and valid claims for damaged, wrong, or defective items are refunded in full, the restocking fee for the Groceries category is set at **0** percent ($0.00). Agents shall never assess a restocking, handling, or processing fee to a grocery transaction.

### 5.0 PERISHABLE, CHILLED, AND HAZARDOUS HANDLING RULES
* **5.1 Photo-Based Resolution:** A grocery item that is damaged, expired, or wrong is refunded strictly on the basis of a verified photograph provided by the customer. A physical return of the item is **never** required.
* **5.2 Prohibition of Collection:** Perishable and chilled items are **never** collected by couriers or logistics partners. Agents are strictly prohibited from generating return labels, scheduling carrier pickups, or instructing customers to mail back perishable or chilled goods under any circumstances.

### 6.0 WARRANTY POSITION
* **6.1 No Warranty Coverage:** No warranty applies to the Groceries category. 
* **6.2 Listing Errors:** Any warranty period, guarantee term, or lifespan representation shown on a listing within the Groceries category constitutes a system or merchant listing error and grants the buyer **0** days of warranty cover. Agents must inform customers that consumable grocery goods carry no post-delivery warranty.
