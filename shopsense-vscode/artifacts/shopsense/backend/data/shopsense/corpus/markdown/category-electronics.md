# Category Policy Addendum: Electronics

**KARTWAY INTERNAL OPERATIONS MANUAL**
**SECTION 4: CATEGORY-SPECIFIC POLICIES**
**ADDENDUM 4.8: ELECTRONICS**

**4.8.1 Scope and Precedence**
This addendum governs all customer operations workflows, return processing, and dispute resolution for items listed under the Electronics category on the Kartway marketplace. Where provisions in this addendum conflict with the general return policy (Section 2), the specific rules in this Addendum 4.8 shall take precedence.

**4.8.2 Return Window**
(1) The standard return window for all Electronics items is strictly 15 days from the recorded delivery date. 
(2) Return requests initiated on day 16 from delivery or later shall be automatically rejected by the Kartway fulfillment system, except where explicitly overridden by Section 4.8.5.

**4.8.3 Condition Requirements and Original Packaging**
(1) Original packaging is mandatory for all Electronics returns. The item must be returned in its original manufacturer box, complete with all internal inserts, protective sleeves, and sealing labels intact.
(2) If an item is returned for a change of mind after being opened, it carries a mandatory restocking fee of 15% of the total amount paid by the customer.
(3) The return pickup fee for a change of mind return is 99 rupees, which shall be deducted directly from the final refund amount disbursed to the customer.

**4.8.4 Damaged, Wrong, or Defective Items**
(1) Any Electronics item received in a damaged, incorrect, or defective state must be reported to Kartway customer operations within 15 days of delivery.
(2) Validated claims under this clause are refunded in full to the original payment method with zero restocking fees and zero return pickup deductions.

**4.8.5 Warranty Position**
(1) Electronics products carry the exact warranty period shown on their individual product listing page. 
(2) Permitted warranty durations are strictly limited to none, 6 months, 12 months, or 24 months.
(3) The warranty validity period is counted strictly from the recorded delivery date. Claims falling outside the stated listing warranty shall be redirected to the manufacturer service network.

**4.8.6 Hazardous Materials and Safety Handling Rules**
(1) Items containing lithium batteries are classified as hazardous for transport and must be collected by surface transport only. Air-freight return routing is prohibited for lithium battery items under any circumstance.
(2) Any report of a swollen, overheating, or smoking battery must be treated immediately as a product-safety report and escalated to the Kartway Safety Incident Team. Under no circumstances shall a swollen, overheating, or smoking battery be processed as a routine customer return.
