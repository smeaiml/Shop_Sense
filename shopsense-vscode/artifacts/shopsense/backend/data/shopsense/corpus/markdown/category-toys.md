# Category Policy Addendum: Toys

# KARTWAY OPERATIONAL POLICY ADDENDUM: TOYS CATEGORY
**Document ID:** KW-POL-CAT-042  
**Effective Date:** 14 January 2025  
**Applies To:** Customer Operations, Seller Support, Logistics Operations  

---

## 1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
This addendum establishes mandatory handling, return, refund, and warranty procedures for all items listed under the Toys category on the Kartway marketplace. Where provisions in this addendum conflict with the Kartway General Marketplace Policy (KW-POL-GEN-001), the rules contained herein shall supersede.

## 2. RETURN WINDOWS AND ELIGIBILITY
2.1. **Standard Return Window:** The return window for all Toys category items is strictly 20 days from the recorded delivery date. Return requests initiated on or after day 21 shall be automatically rejected by the Kartway returns management system.  
2.2. **Damaged, Defective, or Incorrect Items:** Any Toy received in a damaged, defective, or incorrect state must be reported by the customer within 20 days of delivery. Such items qualify for a full refund with zero restocking or pickup fees applied.  
2.3. **Change of Mind Returns:** Customers may return opened items for a change of mind within the 20-day return window, subject to the fee structure defined in Section 5.

## 3. CONDITION REQUIREMENTS AND PACKAGING MANDATES
3.1. **Original Packaging:** Original packaging is strictly mandatory for all Toy returns. The product box, inner blister packs, and manuals must be intact and returned alongside the item. Returns lacking original packaging shall be rejected by the returns warehouse.  
3.2. **Completeness:** All components, accessories, and promotional items bundled with the Toy must be included in the return parcel.

## 4. HAZARDOUS, SAFETY, AND PERISHABLE HANDLING RULES
4.1. **Button Cell Batteries:** Toys containing button cells must be packed and inspected by the seller and fulfillment center such that the battery compartment is taped shut securely to prevent access by children during transit.  
4.2. **Product Safety Incidents:** Any report by a customer that a small part has come loose from a Toy constitutes a product-safety report. Support agents must immediately flag the ticket under safety code `TOY-SAF-09` and escalate to the Kartway Product Safety Team within 2 hours of contact.

## 5. FEES AND DEDUCTIONS FOR CHANGE OF MIND
5.1. **Restoking Fee:** An opened item returned for a change of mind carries a restocking fee of exactly 5% of the total amount paid by the customer.  
5.2. **Return Pickup Fee:** The return pickup fee for a change of mind return is exactly 99 rupees. This amount shall be deducted directly from the final refund disbursed to the customer.

## 6. WARRANTY POSITION
6.1. **Manufacturer and Seller Warranties:** Toys listed on Kartway do not carry a standardized platform warranty. Products carry the warranty period explicitly shown on their individual product listing page—namely none, 6, 12, or 24 months.  
6.2. **Warranty Calculation:** The applicable warranty period is counted strictly from the recorded delivery date. Support agents must verify the listing data via the Kartway Inventory Management System (KIMS) prior to authorizing any warranty claim.
