# Category Policy Addendum: Home & Kitchen

# KARTWAY OPERATIONAL POLICY ADDENDUM: HOME & KITCHEN CATEGORY
**Document ID:** KW-POL-HK-419  
**Effective Date:** 14 January 2025  
**Applies To:** All Kartway Customer Operations Agents, Tier 1–3 Support, and Resolution Specialists  

---

## 1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
This document establishes the binding operational rules for customer operations agents handling returns, refunds, warranties, and item preparation within the Home & Kitchen category on the Kartway marketplace. Where this addendum conflicts with general marketplace guidelines, the rules contained herein shall supersede.

---

## 2. RETURN WINDOWS AND ELIGIBILITY

### 2.1 Standard Return Window
1. **Window Duration:** The standard return window for all Home & Kitchen products is **30** days from the delivery date. No extensions shall be granted beyond the **30**-day threshold.
2. **Damaged, Defective, or Incorrect Items:** Any item received in a damaged, defective, or incorrect state must be reported to Kartway customer operations within **30** days of delivery to qualify for full remediation under Section 3.

---

## 3. REFUNDS, RESTOCKING FEES, AND DEDUCTIONS

### 3.1 Change of Mind Returns
1. **Restocking Fee:** An opened item returned due to a change of mind shall incur a restocking fee of exactly **10**% of the total amount paid by the customer.
2. **Pickup Fee:** The return pickup fee for a change of mind return is **99** rupees. This amount shall be deducted directly from the final refund issued to the customer.

### 3.2 Damaged, Wrong, or Defective Items
1. **Refund Value:** Items reported as damaged, wrong, or defective within the **30**-day reporting window shall be refunded in full.
2. **Fee Exemption:** Zero restocking fees and zero pickup fees shall be applied to damaged, wrong, or defective item returns.

---

## 4. PACKAGING REQUIREMENTS
1. **Original Packaging:** Original packaging is **not** mandatory for Home & Kitchen returns. Customers may return items in alternative sturdy packaging provided the contents are adequately protected during transit.

---

## 5. HAZARDOUS, PERISHABLE, AND SPECIAL HANDLING RULES

### 5.1 Cookware and Appliances
1. **Cleaning Requirement:** All cookware and kitchen appliances must be thoroughly cleaned and completely dry prior to the scheduled return pickup. Agents must advise customers that uncleaned items will be rejected by the courier agent.

### 5.2 Knives and Sharp Blades
1. **Sheath Requirement:** All knives and sharp blades must be returned encased in a dedicated blade guard or their original protective sheath to prevent injury to logistics personnel. Unsheathed blades are strictly prohibited from collection.

---

## 6. WARRANTY POSITION
1. **Warranty Period:** Home & Kitchen products do not carry a uniform warranty. Products carry the exact warranty period explicitly stated on their individual product listing page (**0**, **6**, **12**, or **24** months).
2. **Commencement Date:** All warranty periods are counted strictly from the confirmed delivery date.
3. **Out-of-Warranty Claims:** Claims submitted after the expiration of the listed warranty period (**0**, **6**, **12**, or **24** months from delivery) must be rejected by support agents, and customers directed to the manufacturer where applicable.
