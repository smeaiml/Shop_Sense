# Warranty and Replacement Policy

# SECTION 400: WARRANTY AND DEFECT OPERATIONS POLICY

## 401. Scope and Authority
401.1 This policy governs all warranty claims, defect evaluations, and post-purchase remediations for merchandise transacted on the Kartway marketplace. Support agents are strictly bound by the thresholds, workflows, and decision rules detailed herein. No agent has the authority to negotiate outside these terms.

## 402. Warranty Eligibility and Duration
402.1 The applicable warranty period for any item is strictly the duration displayed on the active product listing at the time of purchase, designated exclusively as none, 6 months, 12 months, or 24 months. 
402.2 The warranty period is counted precisely from the confirmed delivery date recorded by the Kartway shipping system.
402.3 Warranties may only be attached to and claimed within the following eligible categories:
(a) Electronics
(b) Home & Kitchen
(c) Sports & Outdoors
(d) Toys
(e) Furniture
402.4 The following categories carry no warranty whatsoever. Any warranty period displayed on a listing for items in these categories is classified as an administrative listing error and grants the buyer no coverage:
(a) Apparel
(b) Beauty
(c) Groceries

## 403. Handling Pathways: Kartway Desk vs. Manufacturer Network
403.1 The operational routing of a warranty claim is determined strictly by the fulfillment channel of the seller of record:
(a) Products originating from Kartway-fulfilled sellers are handled directly by the internal Kartway warranty desk.
(b) Products originating from seller-fulfilled sellers are handled through the manufacturer's authorized service network, with the Kartway support operations team acting solely as the intermediary forwarding the claim.

## 404. Voids and Exclusions
404.1 A warranty claim is automatically voided, and service is refused, under any of the following conditions:
(a) Evidence of unauthorized disassembly, third-party repair attempts, or modification of internal components.
(b) Liquid ingress, moisture damage, or corrosion where the product carries an IP rating lower than the conditions of use.
(c) Physical damage resulting from drops, impacts, incorrect voltage inputs, or use of unapproved accessories.
(d) Normal wear and tear, cosmetic scratching, fabric fading, or consumable component degradation (including batteries past 300 charge cycles).
(e) Tampering with, removal, or alteration of the factory serial number or IMEI label.

## 405. Replace-Versus-Repair Decision Rules
405.1 Support agents must apply the following decision sequence when evaluating an approved warranty claim within the valid warranty period:
(a) **Day 0 to Day 30:** Any mechanical or electrical failure reported and verified within 30 days of the delivery date must be resolved via a direct product replacement.
(b) **Day 31 to End of Warranty Period:** Any failure occurring after 30 days but inside the active warranty period must be routed for repair, except under two conditions where replacement is mandatory:
  (i) The total amount paid for the item was under 1,500 rupees; or
  (ii) The authorized repair cannot be completed within the mandatory turnaround window defined in Section 406.

## 406. Turnaround Commitments and Authorisation Tiers
406.1 Turnaround commitments are strict operational service level agreements:
(a) **Replacements:** A replacement unit must be dispatched from a Kartway or partner fulfillment center within 3 business days of warranty claim approval.
(b) **Repairs:** A repair must be fully completed and the item dispatched back to the customer within 14 business days of receipt at the service facility.
406.2 A replacement item must be authorised at the exact same financial tier as a refund of the amount paid for the original item, matching the base transaction value recorded in the Kartway order system.
