# Category Policy Addendum: Furniture

# KARTWAY OPERATIONAL POLICY MANUAL
## ADDENDUM 402: FURNITURE CATEGORY SPECIFICATIONS
**Effective Date:** 14 January 2025  
**Applies To:** All Tier-1 and Tier-2 Support Agents, Logistics Coordination Team, and Claims Processing Unit  

---

### 402.1 Purpose and Scope
This addendum establishes mandatory handling, return, refund, and warranty rules for all items listed under the Kartway Furniture category. Where this addendum conflicts with general marketplace guidelines, the provisions of this addendum shall supersede.

---

### 402.2 Return Window
1. **Change of Mind:** The standard return window for a change of mind is strictly **7** days from the date of confirmed delivery. Requests initiated on day **8** or later shall be rejected by the system.
2. **Damaged, Wrong, or Defective Items:** Any claim regarding a damaged, incorrect, or defective furniture item must be reported to Kartway Support within **7** days of delivery. Claims filed after **7** days shall be transferred to the manufacturer warranty process under Section 402.6.

---

### 402.3 Condition Requirements and Packaging
1. **Opened Status Definition:** An item shall be classified as "opened" if factory seals are broken, protective wrappings are removed, or if the product has been fully or partially assembled. Any assembled item automatically counts as opened.
2. **Restocking Fee:** An opened item returned due to a change of mind carries a mandatory restocking fee of exactly **20**% of the total amount paid by the customer.
3. **Original Packaging:** Original packaging is **not** mandatory for returns. Customers are permitted to return furniture without the original boxes or cartons, subject to the inspection rules in Section 402.4.

---

### 402.4 Return Pickup Fees and Logistics Handling
1. **Pickup Fee:** For all change-of-mind furniture returns, a return pickup fee of **499** rupees shall be charged to the customer and automatically deducted from the final refund amount.
2. **Fee Exemption:** No pickup or restocking fees shall be applied to returns resulting from carrier damage, manufacturing defects, or incorrect item delivery.
3. **Handler Requirements:** Due to the physical dimensions of furniture stock, every return pickup requires a minimum of **2** handlers on-site.
4. **Booking Lead Time:** Return pickups must be booked at least **48** hours in advance of the carrier dispatch window. Agents are prohibited from promising same-day or 24-hour furniture collections.

---

### 402.5 Hazardous and Perishable Handling Rules
1. **Non-Applicability:** Standard furniture products on Kartway are non-perishable and non-hazardous. 
2. **Exception Handling:** In the event that a furniture item contains integrated chemical components, pressurized mechanisms (e.g., hydraulic gas lifts in office chairs), or electronic sub-assemblies, agents must route the return through the Kartway Specialized Logistics Desk rather than standard parcel intake.

---

### 402.6 Warranty Position
1. **Duration:** Furniture products carry the exact warranty period explicitly stated on their individual product listing page. Valid durations are restricted to **0** (none), **6**, **12**, or **24** months.
2. **Commencement:** The warranty countdown begins strictly on the confirmed delivery date.
3. **Coverage Scope:** The Kartway marketplace acts as an intermediary for warranty claims during the active warranty window. After the expiration of the listing-specified month count, agents must direct customers to the brand manufacturer.
